Portline

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

Portline is an independent app for following Bills of Lading (B/L) and shipping containers. This page explains what happens to information when you use it.

  • No account. You never sign up or log in.
  • No servers of our own. What you add to Portline is stored only on your phone.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking.

What the app stores, and where

B/L PDFs you import, container and B/L numbers, vessel and port details read from them, notes, supplier orders and your settings are kept in the app's private storage on your phone. They are not sent to us; Portline has no server to receive them. If you uninstall the app, this data is deleted with it. Backups and Excel files you export are saved or shared wherever you choose.

Who the app contacts

  • Shipping lines. To check a shipment, the app sends the container or B/L number directly from your phone to that line's public tracking service: Evergreen (ShipmentLink), COSCO SHIPPING Lines, Maersk and MSC. As with any website, they also see your phone's IP address, and their own privacy policies apply. If you enter your own Maersk Consumer Key, it is stored on your phone and sent only to Maersk with those requests.
  • Map. The map screens load map images from CARTO, based on OpenStreetMap data. CARTO receives your IP address and which part of the map is shown.
  • App stores. If you subscribe to Portline Pro, the purchase is made through the App Store (Apple) or Google Play (Google). We never receive your payment details. The app checks with the store whether your subscription is active.

Notifications

Arrival, ETA-change and free-days alerts are created on your phone. No push server is involved.

Children

Portline is a business tool and is not directed at children.

Your choices

You can delete any shipment in the app, or remove everything by uninstalling it. Because we hold no copy of your data, there is nothing for us to export, correct or delete on our side.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about privacy: open an issue at github.com/YiannisSeir/portline-site/issues.
