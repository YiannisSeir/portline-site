Portline

B/L & container tracking, on your phone

Portline reads a Bill of Lading PDF on your phone, creates its containers, and asks the shipping lines for the latest status directly from your phone. There is no account and no server: your shipments stay on your phone.

  • Import a B/L PDF or paste container and B/L numbers.
  • Live status from Evergreen, COSCO, Maersk and MSC.
  • Arrival countdowns, ETA-change alerts and free-days warnings.
  • Free for one B/L with all its containers. Portline Pro removes the limit.
